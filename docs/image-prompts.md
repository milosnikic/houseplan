# Image prompts for ChatGPT

One prompt per room, all built on the same style block so the pictures share one palette and one light colour. The rooms follow `docs/apartment-plan.md` and layout B. If the plan changes, change the prompt for that room.

## How to use them

1. Use one chat for the whole set. ChatGPT keeps the earlier images as reference.
2. Send prompt 0 first. It makes a material board that every later image refers to.
3. For each room, paste the style block, then the room prompt, as one message.
4. Optional: attach the planner's Export PNG and add "The attached plan is a top view with north at the top. Follow its furniture positions."
5. If a picture drifts (cooler light, a grey sofa, a different wood), reply with the correction prompt at the end of this file.
6. Change one thing per message.

Image generators do not respect dimensions. Use these pictures to judge mood and materials. Use the planner to judge fit.

## Style block (paste at the top of every prompt)

```
STYLE, identical for every image in this set:
Photorealistic interior photograph of one and the same apartment.
Materials: light natural oak with a matte finish for the plank parquet floor, the furniture and the vertical slat wall panels. Warm off-white matte walls and ceilings, a soft cream white, never grey and never pure white. Muted olive green, slightly greyed like dried olive leaves, is the only strong colour and appears on upholstery and bedding. Cream and oatmeal textiles in linen, wool and flat-woven jute. Very small accents in terracotta ceramics. Matte black only for thin metal details such as lamp arms and handles. Green houseplants. No other colours, no grey furniture, no chrome, no marble, no glossy surfaces.
Lighting: early evening. Every lamp and LED strip is the same warm white, 2700 K, dimmed and mostly indirect: hidden strips washing walls and panels, small shaded lamps. The little daylight left in the windows is neutral, never blue. One white balance for the whole set: warm but not orange. No mixed colour temperatures, no cool white spots, no coloured light.
Camera: eye level at 150 cm, 24 mm wide-angle lens, vertical lines straight, natural perspective, everything in focus. No people, no text, no watermark. Landscape format, 3:2.
```

## Daylight version

To see a room by day, replace the Lighting paragraph of the style block with this one and keep everything else:

```
Lighting: late morning. Soft neutral daylight through cream sheer curtains, no direct sun patches. All lamps and LED strips are off. One white balance for the whole set: neutral to slightly warm, never blue or grey.
```
